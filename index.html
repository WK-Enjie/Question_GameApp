Testing for Gases

Ammonia (NH₃)
Turns damp red litmus paper blue.
Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
Turns limewater milky (White precipitate formed).
Chlorine (Cl₂)
Bleaches damp litmus paper.
Hydrogen (H₂)
Extinguishes a lighted splint with a "pop" sound.
Oxygen (O₂)
Relights a glowing splint.
